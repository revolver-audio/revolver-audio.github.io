Who does the work

I'm Dimitris Karavasilis, sound engineer and owner of Revolver Recording Studio in Greece. I've been recording, mixing and mastering there since 2008, and for many years I've also taught sound engineering and music technology. Lately a lot of my work is voice.

I built FONI, a free voice-leveling plugin used by podcasters, because I wanted voices to sound steady and natural without a pile of plugins. Your audio gets the same care: real monitoring, real ears, and no "AI button" left to guess.

English and Greek spoken. Files are kept private and deleted 30 days after delivery.

Questions

What if my recording is noisy or echoey?

Most home recordings can be improved a lot. The free sample will show you honestly how far yours can go before you pay anything.

Longer episodes?

Every extra 30 minutes is half the episode price. Video podcasts: audio only for now, delivered ready to drop into your video edit.

Will my audiobook pass ACX?

Every file is measured against ACX's RMS, peak and noise-floor limits and you get the numbers. If a file can't pass because of the original recording, I'll tell you before you pay.

Do you use AI?

I use modern tools where they help, including my own plugin, but every edit and every master is checked by ear in a treated studio.
